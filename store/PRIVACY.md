# Tabstage privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-04_

Tabstage is a browser extension that lets you sort the tabs you choose into statuses and groups in a side panel.

## What Tabstage stores

- The statuses you create (name, color, order).
- The tabs you add to Tabstage: their title, address (URL), icon, and the status or group you put them in.

This data is stored only in your browser's extension storage. Statuses use the browser's sync storage, so if you've turned on sync in your browser, your browser account keeps them in sync across your devices. The tabs you add stay on the device where you added them.

## What Tabstage does not do

- It doesn't send any data to the developer or to any third party. Tabstage has no servers.
- It doesn't read the content of web pages.
- It doesn't track your browsing, show ads, or sell or share data.
- It only knows about the tabs you add to it yourself.

## Permissions

- `tabs`: read the title, address and icon of open tabs, so you can add them and reopen or switch to them.
- `storage`: save your statuses and the tabs you've added.
- `contextMenus` / `menus`: add "Tabstage → Add to…" to the right-click menu.
- `sidePanel` (Chrome): show the Tabstage panel.
- `tabGroups` (optional, asked only when you use it): remove the old " · Status" suffix that Tabstage 0.1 added to native tab group titles. Tabstage never changes native tab groups otherwise.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling Tabstage deletes all of its data. You can also remove tabs and statuses from the Tabstage panel at any time, and export your data from the options page.
